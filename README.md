## 💣 Complete Catalog of Rage-Baiting Components

### 1. 🪰 The Immortal Screen Fly
- **Behavior**: An animated fly (`🪰`) constantly crawls, buzzes, and wobbles across the screen. It has a habit of landing directly on top of buttons you are actively trying to click.
- **Rage Mechanic**: When your cursor gets within 60px, it plays a high-pitched fly buzz (`bzzzzt`) and darts 150px away.
- **The Punishment**: If you somehow manage to click it, a clown horn sounds and **two more flies spawn in its place**!

---

### 2. 🔕 The Fake Discord Notification Ping (`*Bloop!*`)
- **Behavior**: At random intervals (~25s) or triggered via chaos actions, it synthesizes the exact two-tone **Discord notification sound** (523 Hz ➔ 784 Hz sine wave).
- **Rage Mechanic**: Simultaneously spawns a pixel-perfect Discord toast in the top-right:
  - *"@everyone: Who is making all that clicking noise in #general?"*
  - *"@you: Hey, your boss was asking why you're playing games."*
- **Psychological Effect**: Triggers immediate Pavlovian instinct to switch browser tabs to check Discord.

---

### 3. ⏳ The 99% Loading Bar of Doom
- **Behavior**: Completing the first stage triggers a reward loading screen: *"Preparing 1 Million Dollar Prize..."*
- **Rage Mechanic**: The progress bar animates smoothly: `10%` ➔ `50%` ➔ `90%` ➔ `98%` ➔ **`99%`**.
- **The Stall**: It freezes at **99% for 3.5 agonizing seconds** with the label *"Verifying existential worth..."* before crashing with:
  - *"ERROR 0x80085: Loading bar became self-aware. Progress reset to 0%!"*

---

### 4. 🪫 The Fake 1% Battery Warning Modal
- **Behavior**: A high-priority full-screen modal pops up unannounced:
  - *"BATTERY LEVEL: 1% — Your device will shut down in 10 seconds unless you click below to download extra battery power!"*
- **Rage Mechanic**: Clicking the button blares a clown horn and reveals it was a pure panic scare.

---

### 5. 🚨 Rapid Click / Desk-Slam Detector
- **Behavior**: The engine measures clicks per second. If you click more than 4 times in 1.2 seconds (classic rage-clicking):
  - Synthesizes obnoxious squeaky toy sounds!
  - Triggers a violent screen shake (`body.screen-shake`).
  - M.I.S.S.Y. roasts you: *"🚨 RAGE-CLICKING DETECTED! Calm down! The mouse has a family!"*

---

### 6. 🏃 Runaway Close (`✕`) Buttons on Windows 95 Popups
- **Behavior**: Spawns retro Windows error popups (*"HOT LOCAL POTATOES IN YOUR AREA WANT TO CHAT!"*, *"MANDATORY 4,200 COOKIES POLICY"*).
- **Rage Mechanic**: When you hover over the red/gray `✕` close button, it physically jumps 80px away from your cursor, forcing you to chase it.

---

### 7. 🚨 The "MAKE IT WORSE!" Siren Button
- **Behavior**: A flashing red button in the top marquee that systematically dismantles the website:
  - **Press 1**: Screen tilts 4° + fake speed limit warning.
  - **Press 2**: Screen tilts -6° + popup ad barrage.
  - **Press 3**: Colors invert into blinding radioactive negatives + 1% battery scare.
  - **Press 4**: Screen tilts 10° + Disco Rainbow mode + 8-bit Rickroll synth blares!
  - **Press 5**: 1,000% Annoyance reached!

---

### 8. 🔴 The Hydra Red Button
- **Behavior**: A giant button labeled **"DO NOT PRESS"**.
- **Rage Mechanic**:
  - Click 1: Docks 100 points (*"Status: I literally asked for one thing"*).
  - Click 2: Shifts 120px sideways.
  - Click 3: Flips upside down and sets score to `"NaN"`.
  - Click 4: Multiplies into 5 Hydra decoys (*"Real Button (Honest)"*, *"Free Pizza! 🍕"*, *"I am a Potato 🥔"*). Clicking decoys spawns even more mini-buttons!

---

### 9. 🥔 Dodge Adulting (Inverted Controls & Trap Gold)
- **Behavior**: Canvas action game where you play as Sir Couch Potato 🥔.
- **Rage Mechanic 1**: Every ~6 seconds, **CONTROLS INVERT**! Left becomes Right, Right becomes Left.
- **Rage Mechanic 2**: Shiny bags of gold `💰` fall from the sky... but catching them is a trap! It's a 30-year student loan that drains -35 HP while a sad trombone plays.

---

### 10. ⚡ The Impossible Wire Maze
- **Behavior**: Wireframe canvas game where you must guide your cursor through a narrow labyrinth.
- **Rage Mechanic**: Touching a wall instantly plays a loud buzzer and resets you to the start. Reaching the goal makes the goal vanish and informs you it has taken emotional leave.

---

### 11. 🎚️ The Seesaw Volume Slider from Hell
- **Behavior**: A volume slider on a physical beam with real gravity physics.
- **Rage Mechanic**: You must set volume to 50%. The moment you let go of the ball, any slight tilt causes it to roll uncontrollably to 0% (mute) or 100% (ear damage).

---

### 12. 🔏 Password Verification from Heck
- **Behavior**: You must create a password to claim your high score.
- **Rules**:
  - Rule 1: Must be at least 6 characters.
  - Rule 2: Must contain an emoji.
  - Rule 3: Must contain a Roman Numeral (V, X, L, C, D, M).
  - Rule 4: Must contain Antarctica's current temperature (`-58`).
- **The Trap**: Pressing **Backspace** has a 40% chance of adding random extra letters instead of deleting them!

---

### 13. 🎒 Self-Sabotaging Inventory
- 🍕 **Cold Pizza**: +25 HP, but violent greasy screen shake!
- ☕ **Quadruple Espresso**: Screen jitter + 777 points!
- 🧦 **Missing Sock**: Plays cartoon boings and gets lost in the laundry dimension!
- 📱 **Airplane Mode**: Turns the entire screen pitch black for 2 seconds (*"You asked for no distractions!"*).

---

### 14. 🔊 Web Audio Troll Soundboard

- 🤡 **Clown Horn**: Dual-tone honk honk
- 🎺 **Sad Trombone**: 4-note failure descending slide
